- **Design notes (1-2 paragraphs)**: Stage B is the LLM-assisted complement to v8.0.0 P-12's Stage A heuristic. This 5-condition gate keeps the cost surface predictable: typical 50k-token long-context dispatches hit Stage B selectively, costing ~$0.03 with ~24% additional token savings on top of Stage A's ~12%. The cost ceiling integration with `gate/budget.py` (P-03 deliverable in v8.0.0) means Stage B inherits the same per-dispatch budget tracking as the rest of the v8.x cycle's cost discipline. Open questions deferred per Stage B design §10 (per-artifact vs whole-dispatch invocation, entity sub-sampler, directed_compact ordering, model selection algorithm, output caching) are non-blocking for PV-01 — all 5 are captured in the design doc and should be addressed by an ADR refresh when Stage B reaches PR 4 (default-on flip in v8.2.x).
