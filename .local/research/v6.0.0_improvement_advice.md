## 0. Executive Summary

DevolaFlow v5.4.2 is a mature, well-tested meta-framework (818 tests / 91.07% coverage / 100% lint+docstring). Its **code quality is excellent**, but three structural debts persist:

1. **Dead-wire bug**: `merge_reinforcement_into_dispatch` and `apply_round_escalation` are fully implemented and tested, but **never called in production** — tests exist without runtime integration. These are v5.1-roadmap items (P4, P8) that slipped from their release windows.
2. **Deferred deprecation**: `evaluate_gate_with_nines` / `run_nines_advisor` still emit 12 DeprecationWarnings per test run; they were promised for v6.0 removal (P9) but still ship.
3. **Aspirational adapter proposal never shipped**: The v4.2→v5.0 integration proposal planned 16+ adapters via `AdapterRegistry` + data-driven YAML engine. v5.0 shipped *different content* (NineS v2); the adapter expansion never happened. DevolaFlow still ships only 4 platforms while the ecosystem has grown to 12+.

**v6.0.0 thesis:** *Close the last-mile gap on existing-but-unwired infrastructure, honor the v6.0 deprecation promise, and execute the long-deferred adapter expansion.*

Out of **11 candidates** analyzed with before/after benchmarks, **10 show measurable improvement** (1 negative result — V6-04 `unwanted_hints` — deferred to v6.x). Total estimated v6.0.0 LOC delta: **−420 removed** + **+1,200 added** for structural gains, with user-visible benefits: 0 DeprecationWarnings, 25 scenarios gain regression baselines, 2–4 new adapter platforms, 20% budget elasticity on late convergence rounds.

## 8. Conclusion

v6.0.0 is an **unusually disciplined release candidate**: every proposed change has either (a) explicit v6.0 roadmap documentation going back to v5.1, (b) benchmarked measurable improvement, or (c) explicit deferral with rationale. The critical insight from the analysis is that two of the most expensive pieces of v5.1-5.3 infrastructure — reinforcement injection and round-based context escalation — **ship with tests but without runtime integration**. Closing that dead-wire gap is the single highest-ROI intervention in v6.0.0.

Combined with honoring the long-deferred deprecation promise (P9-P10) and finally executing the v4.2→v5.0 multi-platform adapter proposal that slipped, v6.0.0 has a clear, defensible, benchmarked scope.

**Recommendation:** Proceed to wave planning and implementation per §6. Target release: **v6.0.0 stable** (not pre-release) once Wave 1-5 complete and all SI-10 pre-commit checks pass on the release branch.
