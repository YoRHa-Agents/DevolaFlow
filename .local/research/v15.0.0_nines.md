## §4 — Known upstream artifacts (interpret scores accordingly; unchanged from v14.2.0 run)

* **`code_coverage = 0.0` is a NineS harness bug, not real coverage.** The NineS-internal
  `pytest --cov` sub-run times out at its 54s budget-derived ceiling (stderr verbatim:
  `pytest --cov timed out after 54.0s (budget-derived)`) and targets `cov_package: nines`
  rather than `devolaflow`. DevolaFlow's actual coverage is enforced ≥ 80% by the S-3 floor
  (`pyproject.toml [tool.coverage] fail_under = 80`); the cycle-close measured figure is
  recorded in `v15.0.0_evaluation.md` §3.3. This artifact remains the dominant drag on
  `hygiene_mean` / `weighted_overall`. Tracked upstream in the G-041 parking lot.
* **Markdown is not analyzed.** NineS only indexes Python under `--src-dir`; the
  `workflow-system/agent/` SKILL/reference/template surfaces — where most of the v15-cycle's
  work landed — are invisible to the self-eval. Scores reflect the Python package only.
* Informational stderr (benign, same as v14.2.0): `run_all() called with ctx=None but 1
  ctx-aware dim(s) registered (['decomposition_coverage']); they will fall back to their
  constructor-time src_dir defaults` — the fallback default IS `src/devolaflow`.
