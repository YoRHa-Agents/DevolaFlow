6. **G-018 — v9.0.1 cycle-archive no-op confirmation** (LOW priority): v9.0.1 is a PATCH within the v9.0.0 cycle, so W-19 archive is correctly delayed to cycle close. No action needed; document as intentional no-op in the v9.2.0 retrospective's W-19 section.
