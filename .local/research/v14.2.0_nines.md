## §4 — Known upstream artifacts (interpret scores accordingly)

* **`code_coverage = 0.0` is a NineS harness bug, not real coverage.** The NineS-internal
  `pytest --cov` sub-run times out at its 54s budget-derived ceiling (stderr:
  `pytest --cov timed out after 54.0s (budget-derived)`) and additionally targets
  `cov_package: nines` rather than `devolaflow`. DevolaFlow's actual coverage is enforced
  ≥ 80% by the S-3 floor (`pyproject.toml [tool.coverage]`). Same artifact was recorded for
  v14.0.0/v14.1.0 (see `docs/cycle-archive/v14.1.0/evaluation/v14.1.0_impl_evaluation.md` §4).
  This artifact is the dominant drag on `hygiene_mean` / `weighted_overall`.
* **Markdown is not analyzed.** NineS only indexes Python source under `--src-dir`; the
  `workflow-system/agent/` SKILL/reference/example markdown surfaces (the bulk of DevolaFlow's
  agent-facing behaviour) are invisible to the self-eval. Scores reflect the Python package only.
* Additional stderr note (informational): `run_all() called with ctx=None but 1 ctx-aware dim(s)
  registered (['decomposition_coverage']); they will fall back to their constructor-time src_dir
  defaults` — benign; the fallback default IS `src/devolaflow`.
