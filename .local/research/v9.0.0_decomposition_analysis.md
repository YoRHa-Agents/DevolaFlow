## Executive Summary

**Scope & methodology** *(SYNTHESIS)*: This artifact merges three predecessor SI-1 analyses — `.local/research/v9.0.0_overlap_matrix.md` (141 lines, 14 feature-family lineages + 7 detailed findings + 10 consolidation candidates), `.local/research/v9.0.0_conflict_register.md` (276 lines, 14 conflicts × 4 severity tiers + dependency graph), and `.local/research/v9.0.0_perf_trajectory.md` (330 lines, 7 KPI tables + 10 plateau/breakthrough events + 13 v9.0.0 numeric targets) — into a single decomposition surface for the v9.0.0 cycle. Headline arithmetic: **14 overlap families** (3 LAYERED-WITH-DEAD-WIRE / 2 REDUNDANT / 9 LAYERED-COHERENT / 0 CONFLICTING / 0 CLEAN) × **14 design-axis conflicts** (3 BLOCKER / 5 CRITICAL / 4 MAJOR / 2 MINOR) × **10 plateau / breakthrough events** across the 17-calendar-day, 82-version window v2.1.0 → v8.4.0. The dominant pattern across all three artifacts is *additive surface forever — composable, never retired*; v8.4.0 has now touched three independent ceilings simultaneously (cache-layout `canonical_order` length 16, SKILL.md 499/500, rule taxonomy 50), and v9.0.0 must address the ceilings themselves rather than thread another micro-compression through.

**Proposed 8 named consolidation themes** *(SYNTHESIS)*: **(T1)** SKILL.md compression PV-01 to reclaim ≥ 20-line headroom before any other SKILL surface edits; **(T2)** formalize the v8.4.0 single-source-of-truth registry pattern as Architecture Rule A-5 with parity test against `DEFAULT_ALLOWLIST`; **(T3)** unify the 3 compression entry surfaces (`compressor.py` + `llm_client.py` + `shell_proxy/commands.py`) under a `CompressionPipeline` protocol with stage-level bypass conditions; **(T4)** wire `lifecycle.run_hooks("pre_dispatch", ...)` at the dispatch-emission point and embed prompt-side governance contracts to close the 3-version dead-wire and break the 5-cycle plan-mode recurrence; **(T5)** flip 5 of 7 v8.0.0 opt-in primitives to default-ON via STRICT/AUDIT profile updates; **(T6)** rebalance the 50-rule taxonomy with per-task-type rule selectivity, a 60-rule cap, and a Soul-set-add governance gate; **(T7)** codify cache-layout governance v2 with a nest-vs-append decision rule and multi-baseline byte-identicality regression test; **(T8)** close the NineS A1-A4 hygiene bundle (cov timeout / agent_overhead warning / index rebuild / golden_test_set refresh) bundled with formal audit-cadence W-rules.
