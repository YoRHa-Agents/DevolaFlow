## Notes

- Sampling treats payloads heterogeneously: handoff envelopes may unwrap a `payload:` / `dispatch:` block; research-artifact embeds are extracted from `yaml` code fences whose first or second top-level key is `hdr:` or `task:`.

- Non-empty = key present AND value is non-null, non-empty string, non-empty list, non-empty dict. `0` and `False` ARE considered non-empty (intentional values).
