# v21.1.0 Retrospective

## Gaps identified

The v21.1.0 SI-1 analysis carried forward the evidence limits from the
v18–v20 feedback and the v21.0.0 close:

- the existing harness did not have matched, replicated real-task outcomes
  for a release-delta claim;
- CLI skill-on/off comparisons lacked authenticated channel evidence;
- per-stage input/output token use and runtime distributions were incomplete;
- representative read-only, tool-heavy, multi-file, and recovery outcomes were
  not available as a completed workload series; and
- historical, per-PV, checklist, reversion, and blocker trends were not
  matched to this cycle.

The cycle's 240-artifact calibration makes the boundary explicit rather than
closing it by assumption. Every artifact is `INSUFFICIENT`: planned 240,
observed 240, completed 0, PASS 0, FAIL 0, and INSUFFICIENT 240. Authentication
evidence is insufficient for Claude, Codex, and Kimi; token usage and
skill-loading observations are missing. Consequently ROI and causal quality
improvement are not established.

## Implemented

- Added the HSC `skill_residency` axis with explicit status, scope,
  observation method, fixtures, provenance, and reason fields. No host is
  incorrectly promoted to `implemented` without a captured or vendor-doc
  fixture.
- Added bounded local CLI probe support for Claude, Codex, and Kimi, including
  relative artifact paths, redacted diagnostics, timeout/unavailable
  normalization, token-use status, runtime status, and runtime
  `skill_loaded` facts.
- Added the ordered calibration matrix for four task classes, two arms, three
  channels, and configurable replicates, plus machine-readable aggregation and
  report-only ROI conclusions.
- Preserved explicit missing-data semantics: absent authentication, tokens,
  skill observations, and matched historical evidence remain
  `INSUFFICIENT`; they are never converted to zero or PASS.
- Added current-cycle ghost coverage in
  `tests/ghost/test_features_v21_1.py` for T7 and T4/calibration/baseline
  contracts. The existing v21.0 ghost audit continues to cover T1, T2, T3,
  and T6.
- Ran the built-in final evaluator and settled
  `.local/telemetry/baselines/harness_baseline_v21.1.0.json`, retaining
  `harness_baseline_v21.0.0.json` and `harness_baseline_v20.1.0.json` in the
  active comparison window.

These changes improve contract observability and release evidence hygiene.
They do not demonstrate that DevolaFlow improves real agent-task quality or
reduces ceremony cost.

## Deferred and insufficient evidence

- **Calibration ROI:** deferred because all 240 artifacts are
  `INSUFFICIENT`; no token, skill-loading, authenticated outcome, or
  reproducible arm comparison exists.
- **Quality causality:** deferred because there is no matched replicated
  workload series and no valid uncertainty-backed release comparison.
- **Cross-channel skill residency:** deferred because the current HSC entries
  are designed or undeclared and have no real residency fixtures.
- **Stage performance budgets:** deferred because the available wall-time
  observations are timeout-shaped or suite-level, not a validated per-stage
  p50/p95/p99 task series.
- **Historical and per-PV trends:** deferred because the ledger is not a
  matched v20.1.0/v21.1.0 series and does not support reliable checklist,
  reversion, blocker, or test-function split deltas.

No quality-improvement, latency-improvement, token-saving, or skill-loaded
claim is made from the calibration or the evaluator composite.

## Key learnings

1. A complete matrix of unavailable executions is evidence of coverage and
   availability boundaries, not a successful experiment.
2. Runtime booleans such as `skill_loaded` must remain facts with provenance;
   installation delivery or an arm name cannot substitute for observation.
3. Current repository readiness and causal task quality are separate claims.
   A `READY` built-in evaluator result does not override missing historical or
   real-task evidence.
4. Baseline settlement is useful only when the source evaluator, ledger,
   prior baseline, and unavailable dimensions remain explicit and retained.
5. Stable typed artifacts and ghost audits make the next authenticated
   calibration repeatable without weakening fail-closed behavior.

## Close disposition

The v21.1.0 cycle is engineering-ready only to the extent established by the
final repository gates. Its calibration result is `INSUFFICIENT`, its ROI is
unverified, and its evidence must not be summarized as a quality uplift.
