* **Reconciliation point — Option R-A (NineS overall 9.07 / 10)**: the fresh

  reports `overall=0.907` (capability_mean=0.955, hygiene_mean=0.795,

  `code_coverage=0.0` in NineS's hygiene group. The `capability_mean=0.955`
  is the more honest measure of actual codebase health absent the timeout
  artifact and is consistent with R-B (~0.92).

* **Forecast PV-06 swing target**: **+0.00 ± 0.10 points** (composite must
  re-clear the MAJOR-release threshold ≥ 9.0 / 10 at v12.0.0 cycle close;
  unlike v11.1.0 which forecast +1.65 lift to clear the MINOR threshold from
  a 6.85 baseline, the v12.0.0 cycle's lift is NOT the primary deliverable —
  CORRECTNESS + CLEANLINESS of the BREAKING D-1 + D-2 graduations is).

* **Rationale**:
  1. **Mirrors v11.1.0 PV-01 §1.5 DEC-001 R-B precedent**: lock the W-3 SI-3
     formally-published composite from the predecessor cycle (R-B), not the
     fresh NineS measurement (R-A). The L-4 process improvement explicitly
     codifies this discipline ("every cycle's PV-01 D1 + D2 SI-1 planning gate
     MUST explicitly lock the cycle baseline (W-3 SI-3 OR NineS — pick one
     and document the reconciliation with the other)").

     forecast composite ~9.20 inherits the v11.1.0 close 9.02 + ~+0.18
     subagent-pattern Pattern 3/4 boundary articulation lift. Architecturally
     consistent with the v12.0.0 cycle's continuity from the cascade-restoration
     trajectory.
  3. **R-A is depressed by a measurement artifact**: the `code_coverage=0.0`
     bucket in NineS hygiene group is NOT representative of the actual
     codebase coverage (the v11.4.0 baseline is 93% per
     `docs/cycle-archive/v11.1.0/retrospective.md` §6). Locking against
     R-B avoids embedding the artifact into the cycle's commitment.
  4. **MAJOR-cycle correctness over MINOR-cycle lift**: per §1.3 of the gap
     analysis the predicted v12.0.0 close composite is ~9.18 (above the
     MAJOR threshold by +0.18); the cycle's value-delta is the BREAKING
     graduation cleanliness (D-1 + D-2 + NEW + D-5 conditional), not a
     numeric lift. R-B at ~9.20 sets the right "stay-flat" expectation.

* **Constraint on cycle implementation**: v12.0.0 cycle close (PV-06 NineS
  re-run) MUST NOT regress NineS overall **below 9.00**. The pytest --cov
  timeout artifact is carried forward from v11.4.0 (per v11.4.0 retrospective
  §4.6 Stage-3 shell-tooling failure documentation) as a known issue; the
  cycle MAY but is NOT REQUIRED to lift the timeout to surface true coverage
  before the PV-06 evaluation.

* **Effect on cycle plan (D3 artifact, PV-01 follow-up)**:
  * §2 PV-06 row inherits forecast composite ~9.18 / 10.
  * §5 readiness criterion (1) inherits "v12.0.0 close composite ≥ 9.0
    AND NineS overall ≥ 9.00 (no regression below R-A measured at PV-01)".
  * §1 prose at top of cycle plan references this DEC-001 verbatim.
