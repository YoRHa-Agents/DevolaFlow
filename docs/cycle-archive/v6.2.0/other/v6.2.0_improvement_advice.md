## 0. Executive Summary

v6.1.0 shipped cleanly (896 tests, 94% cov, 0 lint, NineS stable 0.7405) but three structural opportunities surfaced during the retrospective that were NOT in the original v6.0.0 advice:

1. **NineS score plateau is a tool-config artifact, not a code quality issue.** 5 of 6 zero-scores are caused by NineS looking for `data/golden_test_set/`, `.toml` eval files, and `src/nines/__init__.py` paths that don't exist in DevolaFlow. **HYPOTHESIS VERIFIED:** pointing `nines self-eval` at the upstream NineS golden dir produced overall 0.7405 → **0.8805 (+18.9%)** with capability mean jumping 0.7150 → **0.9150 (+27.97%)**. Zero code changes. Pure config.
2. **Windsurf adapter WARN is a real bug.** v6.0.4 shipped it knowing `.windsurfrules` (24,625 chars) exceeds its 8,000-char budget. Customers cannot actually install DevolaFlow on Windsurf despite it appearing in the adapter registry.
3. **Retrospective §7 next-iteration items** are well-scoped and benchmark-friendly: plan-mode detection, dead-API CI guard, 3 more Tier-1 adapters (Zed, Cline, Roo Code).

**v6.2.0 thesis:** *Convert the v6.1 SI-8 retrospective inputs into shipped code, fix the Windsurf real bug, and unlock the NineS score plateau via tool-config.*

9 candidates analyzed with benchmarks; **8 show measurable improvement**. Projected aggregate: NineS overall 0.74 → 0.88+, 8 → 11 adapter platforms, 1 real bug fixed, dead-API regression prevention.
