Five Eras at a Glance

DevolaFlow's 28-day arc, decomposed by commits rather than category labels. Each era boundary is anchored to a specific tag.

Era 1

From scaffold to convergence-as-runtime

v0.1.0 → v3.9.2

From “we shipped a skill” to “iteration is itself the runtime mechanism.” Closes when feedback.py + learnings.py give the convergence loop a runtime body.

Era 2

The four layers become load-bearing

v4.0.0 → v6.2.1

Layering goes from “we have layers” to “the layers carry weight.” v6.1.1 breaks a 7-version NineS plateau by audit, with zero src/ edits.

Era 3

The cache-prefix freeze

v7.0.0 → v7.8.0

v7.0.0 freezes the dispatch payload's first 12 fields. The byte-baseline still passes through every later release. v7.8.0 lands the harder lesson: governance must live where the prompt loads.

Era 4

Governance moves into a workspace

v8.0.0 → v9.2.4

v8.3.0 introduces .local/.agent/ on disk plus four hard rules in one cycle. v9.0.0 caps soul rules at 10. v9.1.3 closes G-005 — the workspace finally engaged at runtime.

Era 5

Measurement becomes the planner

v9.3.0 → v10.1.0

Every cycle in this era is driven by an empirical measurement that picks the next intervention. v9.3.0 cProfile finds 96.6% of dispatch wall-clock; LRU cache → 40× speedup. v10.1.0 absorbs “do our docs read like AI wrote them?” — the same arc, applied to prose.
